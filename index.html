Energy accounting (qualitative)

At every act the story keeps the same ledger: energy in = heat conducted toward the plate + heat stored in part and powder + losses to surroundings. Bar widths in the film are illustrative only. They are not efficiencies and not measured splits.

Safeguards

This is a teaching schematic, not operating instructions for hazardous equipment. Do not set up, operate, or modify powder-bed equipment from this page. Follow the equipment manufacturer instructions, facility procedures, powder-handling controls, and trained-personnel requirements that apply to the actual system.

Limits that stay on screen

  • Supports may serve mechanical and thermal roles. Which role dominates depends on the actual design and process, which this schematic does not specify.
  • There is no universal support pattern and no stress-free part. Removal can reveal distortion from residual stress. The film shows that as a risk label, not a prediction for a specific part.
  • Powder is drawn as a weaker parallel heat path in qualitative terms only. No conductivity, temperature, or time values are asserted.
  • Camera moves, palette, cutaways, and timing are original illustrative directions, not experimentally validated observations.
